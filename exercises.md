Practical exercises are integrated with the lectures. They will give you experience with user research, interaction design, HTML/CSS prototyping, heuristic evaluation, and think-aloud usability testing.

Each exercise must be completed independently unless its instructions explicitly state otherwise. Exercise briefs, submission requirements, and deadlines will be published in [TeachCenter]({{ site.teachcenter_url }}).

## What to expect

- Analyse an interface in terms of users, tasks, and context.
- Develop and refine an interaction design.
- Build a small prototype with modern HTML and CSS.
- Apply a usability inspection method.
- Plan and run a think-aloud test, then communicate your findings.

Keep clear notes on your process, decisions, and evidence. Your submissions should make it possible to understand both the result and the reasoning behind it.
