Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) is the study and practice of designing technology around people: their goals, abilities, contexts, and values. This course introduces the foundations of usable, useful, and thoughtful interactive systems through lectures and integrated practical exercises.

The course combines conceptual foundations with hands-on design work. You will learn how to investigate users and their needs, develop and prototype interaction ideas, and evaluate interfaces using established inspection and testing methods.

The course is offered in the summer semester 2027 at TU Graz as [INH.02021UF — Human-Computer Interaction]({{ site.course_catalog_url }}). It is taught in English by the Institute of Human-Centred Computing as a lecture with integrated exercises (1 lecture / 2 exercise hours). Assessment is continuous.

### Teaching team

**[Alexander Lex](https://www.visdesignlab.net/team/lex/)** is Professor of Human-Computer Interaction at TU Graz’s Institute of Human-Centred Computing and directs the Visualization Design Lab. His research designs, builds, and studies interactive visual data-analysis systems, with particular interests in reproducibility, accessibility, and user studies.

**[Abhraneel Sarma](https://abhsarma.github.io/)** is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Human-Centred Computing at TU Graz. His research develops interactive tools and empirical studies that help people reason about uncertainty and reach robust, reliable conclusions from data.
